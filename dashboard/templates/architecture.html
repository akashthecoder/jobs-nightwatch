Why Pub/Sub is in the middle

The obvious design is a loop: for each change, call the agent. That fails badly. If Gemini times out on the fourteenth posting, postings fifteen through forty never get processed, and you are left in an ambiguous half-finished state with no way to know what did and did not run.

One message per change fixes all of it. Each change succeeds or retries independently with backoff, and they process in parallel — three changes complete in about 48 seconds rather than three sequential 35-second runs.

Where the model is, and is not

Only step 9 involves Gemini. Steps 3 through 7 are ordinary Python. That line is deliberate: a hash comparison is faster, cheaper and more accurate than asking a model whether two records differ, and the relevance gate exists so a model call is never spent on a role that was obviously irrelevant.
